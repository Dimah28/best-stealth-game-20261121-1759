VELVET HOUR

You are Wren, a cat burglar with three brass buttons and a quiet pair of shoes. The Gilded Marionette hangs above the stage, and four ushers walk the dark with lanterns. Lift it. Leave unseen.

  • Sneak through the seats and curtains — the cones cannot see you there.
  • Throw a button to send an usher off to look the other way.
  • Mind the footlights — in the light you are seen from across the room.
  • Three strikes and the house wins. Never spotted at all and the night pays double.

WREN'S FIELD NOTES

HOW THE HOUSE WORKS

  • Vision cones are what an usher sees. A dashed edge means the cone ends there.
  • Light matters. In a lamp pool you are spotted from far away; in shadow, only up close.
  • Sneaking is slow and quiet, and the cones barely notice you. Sprinting is fast and loud.
  • Curtains hide you completely. Seat rows hide you only while you sneak.
  • Prop crates block sight — and bodies. Walk around them, not through them.
  • Stage boards creak. Cross them sneaking, or every usher turns around.
  • Buttons (click to throw) clink where they land. Ushers go to look. That is the whole trick.
  • Suspicion is the arc over an usher's head. Full arc, and he runs at you. Three catches end the night.
  • Ghost: finish never spotted and the take pays +$500.
